# Business questions

Questions this project is built to answer, grouped by stakeholder. These
map directly to the views in `sql/analytics/`.

## Fraud analytics

1. How many fraudulent transactions occurred?
2. What is the total amount lost to fraud?
3. Which transaction type has the highest fraud rate?
4. What percentage of transactions are fraudulent?
5. What is the average fraud transaction amount?

## Customer analytics

1. Who are the top 10 customers by transaction amount?
2. Which customers perform the most transactions?
3. What is the average transaction amount per customer?

## Transaction analytics

1. What is the total transaction volume and average amount?
2. Which transaction type is most common?
3. What is the percentage breakdown by transaction type?

## Executive dashboard

- Total transactions and total transaction amount
- Total fraud cases and fraud amount
- Fraud percentage and average transaction amount
